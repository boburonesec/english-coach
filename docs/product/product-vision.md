# Product vision

## Problem

Many learners know more English than they can use spontaneously. In a real conversation they may struggle to structure a longer answer, explain a complex idea, disagree tactfully, ask for clarification, or remain active in a fast professional discussion. Traditional courses often add knowledge without creating enough reason or opportunity to use it.

## Long-term outcome

English Coach helps a learner communicate comfortably in international professional and everyday environments. Success means being able to participate, understand, and express intent—not merely produce error-free exercises.

## Conversation-first philosophy

The product should give the learner a worthwhile reason to use English regularly, then make each conversation improve future conversations. Interesting topics create authentic language use. Focused review and practice turn that use into durable capability.

Communication comes before perfection. The product observes the whole conversation but works on only a few changes that will most improve the learner's ability to communicate. It must not become a grammar checker, generic chatbot, vocabulary deck, or linear English course with conversation added on top.

## Professional and general English

Professional communication is a major need, but it cannot be isolated from general English. Everyday interests create curiosity, range, and regular practice; professional situations create high-value application. Topic selection and coaching should serve both.

## Retention

Curiosity gets the learner in; progress keeps the learner. Conversations should be interesting enough to return to, while visible transfer—using something more naturally in a later context—provides the deeper reason to stay.
