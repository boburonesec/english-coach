# Learning loop

```text
conversation
    ↓
session review
    ↓
high-value learning priorities
    ↓
focused coaching
    ↓
learner-state evidence
    ↓
future recall/reuse
```

A review may produce many observations about clarity, listening, language patterns, or conversational behavior. Showing or practicing all of them would dilute learning, so the coach selects only one to three priorities with the highest expected communication value.

An observation is evidence, not a permanent weakness. Repeated evidence, confidence, recency, and performance across distinct contexts contribute to learner-state beliefs. A single mistake must not directly rewrite those beliefs.

Focused coaching should require active production. Future conversations then recall or reuse the target in different contexts. Stable transfer—not a one-time correct answer—is the signal that the learner can move from primary focus toward strengthening, stable use, and eventually maintenance.
